# ORCA UI/UX audit, second pass (2026-11-15)

Branch `ui/design-system-v2` at `a500c1c` (merged to `main` as PR #6). Measured against the playbook's UI rules (`~/.claude/skills/playbook/modules/ui-ux.md`, ids like [UI-13]) and this project's `MD/rules.md`. Screenshots: `MD/ux_audit/2026-11-15/`. The first audit is `MD/ux_audit.md` (2026-11-06).

## 1. How this was done

- Real stack: the API on :8000 with the stored catalogue (15,970 objects, last refreshed 2026-10-08), Vite on :5173, signed in as the evaluator.
- Every view (Home, Overview, Globe, Conjunctions, Analytics, Data, Reports, sign-in) in Chrome at 1440×900 and 390×844; Home and Analytics also in the light theme.
- A script measured each view in the DOM: headings and landmarks, controls under 24 px, text under 11.5 px, unnamed controls, uppercase text, cards with both a border and a shadow, sideways overflow.
- The playbook's `check-ai-look.sh` over `frontend/src`, plus source counts for each repeated finding.

**Not covered** (silence is not a pass): screen reader; a real touch device; 360 px and 820 px widths; 1.3× text and 300 px zoom; the light theme on Overview, Globe, Conjunctions, Data, Reports; error states with the API down; the Ask ORCA panel, the tour and the hamburger menu (checked on 2026-11-07, not today); reduced motion.

**Passed** (measured today): one `<main>` on every view; titles read "View | ORCA"; a 2 px focus ring on keyboard focus; no sideways page scroll at 390 px; every image has `alt`; no unnamed control; token contrast 182 of 182 pairs; nav centred (0 px off).

## 2. Grade

Light slop with two honesty faults [UI-91]. The structure, tokens, states and copy discipline are good, and most screens do not need redesigning. What keeps it from its best is a set of small things repeated everywhere: status as tinted pills, a circled "i" on every footnote, three ways of writing Pc, 10 px labels, typed arrow glyphs, tiny page titles. Fixing each one as a class (one component, every use) moves the whole app at once. The two honesty faults come first.

## 5. P2: view by view

### Home (`home-light.png`)
- The four KPI cards are equal-weight with an icon each: a template tell [UI-19]. The one that matters (high-risk approaches in the run) does not stand out [UI-26].
- "Close pairs" label vs the spec's "Close-pair encounters"; "Critical approaches 0" sits beside "High-risk 29" as an equal card: one row ("29 high-risk, none critical") says it with less.
- The Earth animation loops forever [UI-19 perpetual motion]. It is the brand moment, so keep it, but consider playing the collision once on load and resting on the scattered frame (reduced motion already shows a still frame).
- Light theme: the glow reads as a grey-violet smudge around the Earth.

### Overview (`overview-1440.png`, `overview-390.png`)
- Three different counts explained in three places: 15,970 stored (KPI), 5,000 loaded (KPI hint and the line under the filter), 250 shown (only at the bottom of the table, after 250 rows). Say "250 of 5,000 loaded (15,970 stored)" once, above the table [UI-06].
- No way to see past row 250: add "Show next 250" or pagination [UI-06].
- Five equal KPI cards: on a phone they stack to 520 px before the first row. Two columns on a phone, or a single compact strip.
- Degree units float: "97.33 °" (a space before °).
- Regime appears twice: as filter chips and as a boxed badge in every row.

### Globe (`globe-1440.png`, `globe-390.png`)
- No h1 (screen-reader users land with no page heading).
- The altitude bands are listed twice: in Filters (with counts) and again in the bottom legend [UI-52 one home per fact].
- "Show conjunction" and "Show only conjunction objects" are disabled with no reason on screen [UI-09].
- "108 object(s) could not be propagated": plural it properly ("108 objects"), and say why in the tooltip.
- Coloured by altitude, 95% of points are the same blue: the colouring carries almost no information at the default zoom. Consider object type as the default, or finer LEO bands.
- Phone: the legend covers the top third of the globe and the catalogue panel sits below the fold; collapse the legend to one line by default.
- `LIVE` is a green tinted pill (see #3).

### Conjunctions (`conjunctions-1440.png`, `conjunctions-390.png`)
- "How this list is made" is five dense columns of method prose at 13 px: hard to scan. Two columns, the plain-language paragraph first, the technical rows behind it.
- Risk tiers as "CRITICAL > 1e-4, HIGH > 1e-5…" (see #5): a small table with the tier word, its dot and its threshold.
- The disclosure uses a typed "▼" (see #7).
- Inputs: "Horizon 1 days" reads as one field; the unit belongs inside the field's label ("Horizon (days)") or as an inline suffix in the input box.

### Analytics (`analytics-1440.png`, `analytics-light.png`)
- KPI "2211 km" has no thousands separator; every other figure has one ("15,970").
- Every chart card carries a circled "i" (see #4).
- Colour reuse: the categorical palette means regime in the donut AND constellation in the bars, so STARLINK is drawn in the LEO blue [UI-31].
- "How fresh is the catalog": while every object is stale the chart is one red bar; say "All 15,970 element sets are over 30 days old" and keep the chart for when it varies.
- "Unnamed (designator only)" wraps to three lines in the bar label column.
- The altitude histogram's x-axis labels are cut at 900 px height.

### Data (`data-1440.png`)
- `TLE 36.6 D OLD`: the unit "d" is uppercased into "D" by the chip, and the chip is a red tinted pill (see #3).
- "ok (2026-10-08 05:23 UTC)": a raw status word. "Succeeded, 8 Oct 05:23 UTC" (the Home panel already says it that way: one formatter, `fetchSummary`).
- "Base URL: same origin": developer jargon; say the actual origin.
- Half the page is empty below two cards; fine, but the refresh action and its result could sit together.

### Reports
- Two h1 on the page (the page and the report preview; see #6).
- Otherwise clean after the side-stripe and marking changes.

### Sign-in (`login-1440.png`, `login-390.png`)
- Glass and backdrop (see #10); the centred floating auth card over an illustration is itself a listed tell [UI-17].
- "Sign in" while disabled looks like any outline button: there is no visible primary action. Make it the primary style, disabled until both fields are filled, with the reason as its hint.
- The "Fill the sign-in form" control is a round icon sitting on the divider between the two credential rows; its purpose cannot be read. A labelled text button ("Use these details").

### Shared chrome
- Phone top bar: six tabs in a sideways-scrolling row; "Data" and "Reports" are off-screen with no cue that the row scrolls. A fade at the right edge, or a bottom tab bar on phones.
- Phone status bar: cut off mid-word ("LAST API REF") with no cue; on a phone show two readings and the rest in the menu.

## 6. Proposed direction (to approve before the polish pass) [UI-92]

- **Product:** an educational space-situational-awareness instrument for evaluators and students, built on public TLEs.
- **Character:** exact, sober, accountable.
- **World:** flight-dynamics consoles and printed conjunction data messages (CDMs): tabular figures, UTC everywhere, provenance on every number.
- **Moments:** the Earth on Home, and a single conjunction's detail. Everything else stays quiet.
- **Signature:** the provenance stamp. Every figure says where and when it came from ("stored example run · 6 to 7 Nov", "catalogue as of 8 Oct"), in one consistent small form, the way the report's handling marking does for the whole document.

## 7. Fix plan, in the playbook's order [UI-94]

1. **P0 honesty** (#1, #2): Home headline tense, stale strip on Home, past-window wording for the run. Small, no new components.
2. **Colour and status** (#3, #11): `Badge` to dot + word; regime as text; Globe chrome elevation.
3. **Icons and glyphs** (#4, #7): drop the `FieldNote` icon; SVG for every arrow and caret.
4. **Copy and numbers** (#5 and the P2 copy items): one `<Pc>` atom; thousands separators; units; plurals; "Succeeded"; real origin.
5. **Type** (#6, #8, #12): page-title scale, 11 px floor, sentence-case titles.
6. **Layout** (P2 per view): Overview counts and paging, phone KPI grid, Globe duplicate legend, Conjunctions method, phone nav cue.
7. **Details and targets** (#9, disabled reasons, sign-in controls).
8. **Motion and decoration** (#10, Earth loop): needs your yes, because it removes looks you may like (the sign-in backdrop, the hero glow).

Each step lands with a check where one is possible (a rules test for tinted badges, typed glyphs, sub-11 px text and missing h1), before and after screenshots, and the MD files updated in the same commit.
